# Module Summary

- Products: categories, normalized modules/features/highlights/requirements/gallery/pricing/FAQ/SEO, publishing and public catalogue.
- Services: categories, capabilities, process, technologies, gallery, FAQ/SEO and public catalogue.
- Portfolio: categories, case-study content, gallery, technologies, outcomes and SEO.
- Blog: categories, tags, authors, scheduling, downloads, FAQ, related content, RSS and SEO.
- Communication Hub: contact messages, demo and quote requests, assignment, replies, private attachments, notes, notifications and quote-to-order conversion.
- Orders: customer/order items, delivery and payment workflow, notes, events, private attachments and optional Finance synchronization.
- Money Management: income/expense categories, transactions, private attachments, reports, CSV/PDF export and dashboard statistics.
- Media Library: folders, optimized images, private documents/video, previews, duplicate detection, replacement, restore, usage tracking, storage statistics and cross-module reuse.
- Website CMS and Settings: homepage/about/contact, navigation, footer, branding, social, SEO, analytics, email and maintenance controls.
- Administration: profile, notifications, Activity Log, dashboard and System Health.
